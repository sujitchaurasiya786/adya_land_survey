About Our Director

The mind behind
every measurement

"A survey is not just numbers on paper — it is the foundation on which families build their futures and industries plant their roots."

Er. Arvind Chaurasiya is a licensed civil engineer and government-certified land surveyor with over 15 years of hands-on experience across residential, commercial, and government projects in Uttar Pradesh. Based in Lucknow, he founded Adya Land Surveying & Design with a clear mission: to bring precision, transparency, and professionalism to every boundary determination, topographic study, and design engagement.

From large-scale government infrastructure surveys to sensitive property boundary disputes, Er. Chaurasiya's reports have been cited in judicial proceedings and trusted by developers, municipalities, and individual landowners alike. He combines traditional surveying expertise with modern GPS and Total Station technologies to deliver legally sound, clearly presented reports.

What We Do

Complete land survey
& design solutions

From boundary marking to full design deliverables — Adya handles every step with precision and professionalism.

📐

Land Boundary Survey

Accurate demarcation of property boundaries using GPS and Total Station. Court-admissible reports with full legal documentation.

🗺️

Topographic Mapping

Detailed terrain maps capturing elevation, contours, and land features — essential for civil construction and infrastructure planning.

🏗️

Layout & Site Planning

Precise on-site layout marking for construction projects, colonies, and commercial developments. Verified against approved plans.

📋

Revenue & Khasra Survey

Khasra number verification, mutation support, and revenue land record surveys aligned with UP government standards.

🎨

Architectural Design

Building plans, site layouts, and design drawings prepared by our in-house design team — ready for municipal approval submission.

📄

Survey Reports & Docs

Professionally formatted survey reports, title verifications, and geo-referenced maps for banks, courts, and government bodies.

How We Work

Our proven
four-step process

Every survey follows a rigorous methodology — accurate, documented, and delivered on schedule.

01

Site Briefing

We study your land records, Khasra maps, and project requirements before boots hit the ground.

02

Field Survey

On-site measurement using GPS, Total Station, and drone-assisted tools for maximum precision and coverage.

03

Data Processing

Survey data is processed, verified, and cross-checked against official records for error-free output.

04

Report & Handover

Final maps, legal reports, and design files handed over with a detailed walkthrough and archival copies.
