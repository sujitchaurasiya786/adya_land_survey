What We Offer

Core Services

Land Boundary Survey

Precise demarcation and documentation of land boundaries using GPS-enabled total station equipment, fully compliant with UP Revenue Department norms.

Topographic Survey

Detailed contour mapping and elevation analysis for construction planning, drainage design, and infrastructure development projects.

Site Planning & Layout

Master layout marking, plot sub-division, and site setting-out services for residential colonies, commercial complexes, and industrial zones.

Contour Mapping

High-precision 3D terrain modelling and contour generation using drone photogrammetry and conventional total station methods.

Legal Compliance Reports

Preparation of survey reports, mutation documents, and demarcation certificates recognised by UP state revenue courts and authorities.

Urban & Rural Planning

Integrated land-use planning, zoning analysis, and development layout design for municipal bodies and private developers across UP.

Meet the Director

"Every parcel of land tells a story. Our job is to read it with precision — and then write its future with integrity."

Er. Arvind Chaurasiya is the founder and driving force behind Adya Land Surveying & Design. A licensed engineer with deep roots in Uttar Pradesh's land administration ecosystem, he established the firm with a singular mission: to bring modern surveying technology and rigorous professional ethics to property development across the region.

With over a decade of hands-on experience spanning boundary surveys, topographic mapping, urban layout planning, and revenue court documentation, Er. Chaurasiya has built a reputation for uncompromising accuracy and client-first service across Lucknow and surrounding districts.
